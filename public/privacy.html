Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Pixel Proof is designed with privacy as a core principle. All image analysis happens locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit your images to any server.

Data Collection

Pixel Proof does not collect any personal data. The extension operates entirely within your browser:

  • Images are analyzed locally using Web Workers
  • No images are uploaded to external servers
  • No account or registration is required
  • No analytics or tracking scripts are used

Local Storage

The extension stores analysis history locally on your device using IndexedDB. This data:

  • Never leaves your browser
  • Can be cleared at any time through the extension settings
  • Is not accessible to any external service

External Services

When you use the reverse image search feature, you may be redirected to third-party search engines (Google, Bing, TinEye, Yandex). These services have their own privacy policies that apply when you use them.

Permissions

The extension requests only the minimum permissions necessary:

  • activeTab: To analyze images on the current page
  • contextMenus: To add right-click analysis option
  • storage: To save your preferences and analysis history locally

Updates

This privacy policy may be updated occasionally. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or the extension, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
