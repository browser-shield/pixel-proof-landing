FAQ

frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Pixel Proof

How does AI image detection work? +

We analyze frequency patterns, noise signatures, and compression artifacts unique to different AI generators like Midjourney, DALL-E, and Stable Diffusion.

Is my data really private? +

Yes! All image analysis happens locally in your browser using Web Workers. Images are never uploaded to any server. Your data stays on your device.

What is Error Level Analysis (ELA)? +

ELA reveals areas of an image that have been manipulated by analyzing JPEG compression artifacts. Edited regions show different error levels than the original image.

What is C2PA verification? +

C2PA (Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity) is a standard for verifying the origin and history of digital content through cryptographic signatures.

How accurate is the AI detection? +

Our detection achieves high accuracy on major AI generators. However, we recommend using multiple analysis methods together for the most reliable results.
